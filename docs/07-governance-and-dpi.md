## 1. DPI Alignment: The National Public Search Layer

In India's Digital Public Infrastructure framework, **Unsung India Search** serves as the sovereign search and knowledge index for national memory, culture, and educational discovery:

```
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
|                    INDIAN DIGITAL PUBLIC INFRASTRUCTURE                     |
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
| Identity Layer      | Aadhaar / DigiLocker                                  |
| Payment Layer       | UPI / NPCI                                            |
| Linguistic Layer    | Bhashini (National Language Translation Mission)      |
| Data & Heritage     | Indian Culture Portal + National Archives + OGD       |
| **Search Engine**   | **Unsung India Search (Zero-Latency National Index)** |
+─────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────+
```

---

## 2. Zero-Hallucination & Primary Provenance Policy

To maintain trust as a national asset:
1. **Primary Archive First**: Every person indexed must link to verified historical documentation (National Archives of India, PIB, Indian Culture Portal, CSIR, or academic publications).
2. **Safe Media Only**: Images are restricted to `CC0`, `CC-BY`, `CC-BY-SA`, or verified `Public Domain`.
3. **Open Standards & Free Access**: All endpoints adhere to OpenAPI 3.0 and are free of commercial tracking or paywalls.
